# Champions Oncology

> Champions Oncology (NASDAQ: CSBR) is a global preclinical and clinical oncology contract research organization (CRO) supporting pharmaceutical and biotech drug development from discovery through the clinic. It maintains a living bank of 1,400+ patient-derived tumor models and offers proprietary in vivo/ex vivo platforms, next-generation sequencing, quantitative and phospho-proteomics, flow cytometry, and the Lumin Bioinformatics analytics and visualization platform built on curated multi-omics data from 12,000+ cancer patients. Champions publishes no public developer REST API; its Lumin platform is a no-code web application and its GitHub org hosts research scripts and notebooks.
